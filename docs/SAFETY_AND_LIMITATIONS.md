# Safety and Limitations

Digital Krishna is not a medical diagnosis tool, emergency service, therapist replacement, or divine authority. It offers no guaranteed outcomes. People facing immediate danger or crisis should contact local emergency services and a trusted person; health and other high-stakes concerns require qualified professionals.

The system should not present unsupported exact quotations. When uncertain, it should say so and use verified retrieval where available. Retrieval itself can be incomplete or wrong and must not be treated as infallible.

Privacy principles include data minimization, avoiding unnecessary personal details, and never publishing user conversations. Age-appropriate communication and escalation to trusted adults or qualified professionals are required where relevant.

Known risks include hallucination, inconsistent safety behavior, uneven English/Hindi/Hinglish quality, cultural overgeneralization, contested interpretations, retrieval failures, and limited evaluation coverage. Multilingual fluency does not guarantee cultural correctness. Public evaluation results, when added, describe only the tested set and conditions.

Operational security details are intentionally excluded.
