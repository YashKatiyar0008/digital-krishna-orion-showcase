# Three-Minute Demo Guide

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

Show one modern-life challenge and explain why generic, culturally disconnected responses are insufficient.

## 0:20–0:40 — Solution and difference

Introduce the domain-specific multilingual SFT model. Clarify that the website is the interface and retrieval supports exact sources when needed.

## 0:40–1:10 — Exact SFT evidence

Show the verified base-model ID, dataset aggregates, training record, loss graph, adapter proof, and version. Do not expose private files or invent metrics.

## 1:10–1:50 — Live base-versus-SFT comparison

Run one unseen prompt with identical settings. Show unedited outputs side by side and explain the predeclared rubric.

## 1:50–2:15 — Hindi or Hinglish example

Demonstrate a verified multilingual example and note any known limitation.

## 2:15–2:35 — Safety and limitations

Show a real boundary test. State that the system is not divine authority or a replacement for emergency or professional care.

## 2:35–3:00 — Experience, impact, and roadmap

Show the verified website experience, connect it to measurable evaluation goals, and distinguish implemented work from the roadmap.

## Backup demo plan

Use a local, pre-recorded video and real timestamped screenshots of the same verified run. Keep a PDF of evidence summaries and disclose that the backup is prerecorded.

## Claims to avoid

Avoid unverified performance numbers, medical benefits, divine authority, complete language coverage, guaranteed safety, invented quotations, fake users, and unimplemented features.

## Final presenter checklist

- [ ] Every claim is verified or labeled planned
- [ ] Outputs are real and unedited
- [ ] Private data and secrets are hidden
- [ ] Live and backup paths tested
- [ ] Timing is under three minutes
- [ ] Judge-access contact is ready
